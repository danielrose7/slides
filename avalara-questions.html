Plantiful · Sales Tax Integration

Questions for the Avalara team

Working list for our kickoff conversations. Grouped by topic; each question notes why we're asking, and where we already have a preferred direction it's marked "our lean" so you can confirm or redirect. We've read the developer docs (use-cases + dev guide) — where they already answer something, the question says so and asks only for confirmation.

What we're building: Plantiful is nursery-management software (ERP + POS + e-commerce for plant nurseries). We're integrating AvaTax for sales-tax calculation. Architecture: a single Plantiful Avalara account, with each customer organization provisioned as its own company (sub-account) under it — our customers never hold Avalara credentials. For the initial release Avalara is a calculator only: all calls uncommitted, no committed transactions, no nexus declared in Avalara, registration tracked on our side. We are not pursuing the certification badge initially but want to architect so it's cheap later.

1 · Account model & provisioning

  1. Can we programmatically create a company per customer org under one account via the Companies API? Any limits on company count, or cost per company?

    We expect dozens → hundreds of orgs over time, provisioned automatically when we enable the integration for an org.

  2. Any constraints or conventions we should follow for companyCode naming?

  3. Does the sandbox environment mirror production for the Companies API and multi-company setups, so we can test provisioning end-to-end?

  4. What exactly counts as a billable transaction on our plan? Specifically: are DocType: SalesOrder calls (non-persisted estimates) billed the same as invoice documents? Uncommitted vs committed? Are lookup endpoints (ListTaxCodes, jurisdiction lists, ResolveAddress) free?

    We gate the integration per org partly to control per-transaction cost, and we may skip calls for exempt customers when an org opts out of exempt tracking.

2 · Document types & lifecycle

Initial release is calculator-only; finalized sales are not reported to Avalara.

  1. For calculator-only usage, do you recommend DocType: SalesOrder (not persisted) or uncommitted SalesInvoice (persisted)?

    Determines whether we must issue VoidTransaction when an order is abandoned, deleted, or voided in Plantiful before posting — persisted documents would otherwise orphan on your side.

    Our lean: SalesOrder for MVP — your docs confirm it isn't persisted (id −1, "no cleanup for abandoned carts"), so deletion/abandonment is a no-op. Confirm there's no downside for a future move to committed invoices.

  2. When we later adopt committed transactions, is reusing the same docCode from earlier uncommitted calls the right way to make Avalara treat it as one transaction?

  3. For post-payment amendments (credits, refunds, corrections): when is AdjustTransaction appropriate vs a ReturnInvoice document?

    Nursery orders routinely change after invoicing; we need the compliant pattern before we design credits. Your docs suggest: adjust/void until Locked, RefundTransaction for money returned — confirm that reading.

  4. Tax effective date: our orders carry an order date, ship date, invoice date, and payment date — which should drive the rate as the transaction date? Your docs' convention is invoice date — happy to follow that; is ship date equally acceptable given our records are per-shipment?

    Nursery pre-orders can sit months between order and fulfillment, across rate changes — we want the convention nailed down before we build. Also confirm returns re-rate via TaxOverride: TaxDate to the original sale's date.

  5. Is docCode unique per company, or per company + document type?

    Your use-cases docs say per DocType (a return may reuse the invoice's number); the dev guide says per company. Affects our return-numbering strategy.

3 · Tax codes for the nursery vertical

  1. What are your recommended tax codes for: live plants / nursery stock, seeds, fertilizer, agricultural supplies, generic taxable hard goods, and occasional food/edibles?

    These become the preset options in our catalog "tax classification" dropdown, so growers never see raw codes.

  2. What other non-taxable categories do nurseries or agricultural sellers commonly need that we haven't listed?

  3. Which codes should we use for freight/shipping, delivery drop charges, miscellaneous additional charges, and late fees? Specifically: FR020100 is common carrier, FOB destination — most nurseries deliver on their own trucks; which FR code applies there?

    Your docs show handling as OH010000 (separate line) / FR030000 (combined with freight), and that freight taxability follows the goods, prorated — we'll rely on that behavior.

  4. For an item our customer marks non-taxable: should we send the line with taxCode: NT (or a specific code) or omit the line entirely?

    Our lean: always send with the code, so totals and the per-jurisdiction breakdown stay complete — confirm.

  5. How often does the tax-code list itself change — new codes added, codes deprecated or remapped? Annually? Rarely? Is there a changefeed or notification we should watch?

    Our catalog classifications map to your codes via a hardcoded table; we need to know whether that's a set-and-forget mapping or something to re-verify on a cadence.

4 · Exemptions

  1. Our customer records carry one of 16 exemption reasons. Please confirm the entity/use code for each — our tentative mapping to your standard codes is in parentheses:

    Agricultural production (H) · Charitable organization (E) · Direct mail (K) · Direct pay permit (J) · Educational organization (M) · Federal government (A) · Foreign diplomat (D) · Hospital (— no obvious standard code; E? L?) · Industrial production / manufacturing (I) · Local government (N) · Multiple points of use (MPU?) · Religious organization (F) · Resale (G) · State government (B) · Tribal government (C) · Other (L)

    Our lean: this enum was modeled on your standard code list, so we expect near-1:1 — the ones we need real guidance on are Hospital and Multiple points of use, plus what to send for Other (L verbatim, or a custom code per customer?)

  2. Is sending entityUseCode at the document header plus each line's taxCode the right way to get partial exemption behavior — e.g. an ag-exempt customer still taxed on hard goods, per jurisdiction rules?

    This is the single most important correctness case for our nurseries — and your docs don't settle it. The dev guide reads blanket ("if your customer is exempt, referencing a taxable tax code will still generate a zero tax result"). We need explicit confirmation of the rule-conditional per-line behavior, and we'll verify in sandbox.

  3. Is exemptNo the right field for the certificate/license number we hold as free text? Any format constraints?

  4. Do we need exemption certificate management (ECM/CertCapture) for calculation to behave correctly, or is that only for audit documentation?

5 · Discounts, fees & credits

  1. Discount representation — confirm our reading of your docs: line discounts netted into the line amount; order-level discounts via the header discount field + discounted: true flags (pro-rated); third-party/manufacturer-reimbursed coupons as a negative line with OC030000 (taxed on the pre-coupon base). Anything we're missing?

  2. For store promotions where the nursery itself funds the discount (including POS coupons), we'll treat them as seller-funded (reduce the taxable base) — is that the correct default? What distinguishes a "third-party" coupon in practice?

  3. Line-level fees (a fee attached to a specific line, where the fee may be taxable even when the item is not): own line with the fee's tax code, or folded into the item line?

    Our lean: own line, so a taxable fee on a non-taxable plant is taxed correctly — confirm.

  4. Store credit applied to an order: as a discount (reduces taxable base) vs as a payment (tax-neutral) — is that distinction correct in all states? If a credit-as-discount lands after the invoice is paid, what's the compliant flow?

6 · Sourcing: pickup vs delivery

  1. For customer pickup at the nursery (including POS sales), is the pickup/register location's address the correct jurisdiction source in all states? Any origin-sourcing states where delivery orders also source from our customer's location?

  2. We send header-level ship-from per fulfilling location. Anything else needed for correct sourcing when a nursery later operates multiple locations?

7 · Registration, nexus & home-rule jurisdictions

Registration is configured in Plantiful and synced to each org's Avalara company as nexus declarations (your docs: without declared nexus, calculations return $0).

  1. Which endpoints should we use to (a) source the list of US states and the self-administered / home-rule sub-jurisdictions per state (AL, CO, LA, AK…) for our registration-picker UI, and (b) declare/remove nexus on a company programmatically as the org edits their registration?

  2. When we declare nexus for a home-rule state, can we scope it to specific local jurisdictions so the calculation natively excludes locals the org isn't registered in? Or do we still need to suppress those authorities from the response ourselves?

    If nexus declarations handle this natively, we can drop our planned response-side suppression entirely — much preferred.

  3. For destination states with no declared nexus, your engine returns $0 (full amount as exempt). As a cost optimization we may skip those calls client-side and apply $0 directly — any concerns? Your docs note "you must always call CreateTransaction" so exempt amounts get reported — does that matter for a calculator-only (uncommitted) integration?

8 · Operations & limits

  1. The developer docs publish no SLA, latency, or rate-limit numbers (only error 1723, a concurrency limit). What are the actual limits and latency expectations we should engineer against? What call-ratio-per-order do you consider healthy? (We plan to debounce to roughly 3–5 calls per order.)

  2. Beyond status.avalara.com, is there a machine-readable health signal or webhook? Which error codes do you consider retryable vs terminal? (We'll retry persisted docs via CreateOrAdjustTransaction per your docs.)

  3. Is the official JS SDK (avatax on npm, AvaTax-REST-V2-JS-SDK) the recommended client for Node backends? Anything about its maintenance cadence we should know?

9 · Looking ahead (not blocking)

  1. When we move to committed transactions + declared nexus for remittance/reporting later: anything in the calculator-only architecture above that would make that migration painful?

  2. What does the Sales Tax Calculation Badge / certification path look like for an integration structured as a single partner account with per-customer companies?
