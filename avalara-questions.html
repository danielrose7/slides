Plantiful · Sales Tax Integration

Questions for the Avalara team

Working list for our kickoff conversations. Grouped by topic; each question notes why we're asking, and where we already have a preferred direction it's marked "our lean" so you can confirm or redirect.

What we're building: Plantiful is nursery-management software (ERP + POS + e-commerce for plant nurseries). We're integrating AvaTax for sales-tax calculation. Architecture: a single Plantiful Avalara account, with each customer organization provisioned as its own company (sub-account) under it — our customers never hold Avalara credentials. For the initial release Avalara is a calculator only: all calls uncommitted, no committed transactions, no nexus declared in Avalara, registration tracked on our side. We are not pursuing the certification badge initially but want to architect so it's cheap later.

1 · Account model & provisioning

  1. Can we programmatically create a company per customer org under one account via the Companies API? Any limits on company count, or cost per company?

    We expect dozens → hundreds of orgs over time, provisioned automatically when we enable the integration for an org.

  2. Any constraints or conventions we should follow for companyCode naming?

  3. Does the sandbox environment mirror production for the Companies API and multi-company setups, so we can test provisioning end-to-end?

  4. What exactly counts as a billable transaction on our plan? Specifically: are uncommitted calls billed the same as committed? Are lookup endpoints (ListTaxCodes, jurisdiction lists, ResolveAddress) free?

    We gate the integration per org partly to control per-transaction cost, and we may skip calls for exempt customers when an org opts out of exempt tracking.

2 · Document types & lifecycle

Initial release is calculator-only; finalized sales are not reported to Avalara.

  1. For calculator-only usage, do you recommend DocType: SalesOrder (not persisted) or uncommitted SalesInvoice (persisted)?

    Determines whether we must issue VoidTransaction when an order is abandoned, deleted, or voided in Plantiful before posting — persisted documents would otherwise orphan on your side.

    Our lean: SalesOrder for MVP so deletion is a no-op — confirm there's no downside for a future move to committed invoices.

  2. When we later adopt committed transactions, is reusing the same docCode from earlier uncommitted calls the right way to make Avalara treat it as one transaction?

  3. For post-payment amendments (credits, refunds, corrections): when is AdjustTransaction appropriate vs a ReturnInvoice document?

    Nursery orders routinely change after invoicing; we need the compliant pattern before we design credits.

3 · Tax codes for the nursery vertical

  1. What are your recommended tax codes for: live plants / nursery stock, seeds, fertilizer, agricultural supplies, generic taxable hard goods, and occasional food/edibles?

    These become the preset options in our catalog "tax classification" dropdown, so growers never see raw codes.

  2. What other non-taxable categories do nurseries or agricultural sellers commonly need that we haven't listed?

  3. Which codes should we use for freight/shipping, delivery drop charges, miscellaneous additional charges, and late fees?

  4. For an item our customer marks non-taxable: should we send the line with taxCode: NT (or a specific code) or omit the line entirely?

    Our lean: always send with the code, so totals and the per-jurisdiction breakdown stay complete — confirm.

4 · Exemptions

  1. Our customer records carry one of 16 exemption reasons. Please confirm the entity/use code for each — our tentative mapping to your standard codes is in parentheses:

    Agricultural production (H) · Charitable organization (E) · Direct mail (K) · Direct pay permit (J) · Educational organization (M) · Federal government (A) · Foreign diplomat (D) · Hospital (— no obvious standard code; E? L?) · Industrial production / manufacturing (I) · Local government (N) · Multiple points of use (MPU?) · Religious organization (F) · Resale (G) · State government (B) · Tribal government (C) · Other (L)

    Our lean: this enum was modeled on your standard code list, so we expect near-1:1 — the ones we need real guidance on are Hospital and Multiple points of use, plus what to send for Other (L verbatim, or a custom code per customer?)

  2. Is sending entityUseCode at the document header plus each line's taxCode the right way to get partial exemption behavior — e.g. an ag-exempt customer still taxed on hard goods?

    This is the single most important correctness case for our nurseries.

  3. Is exemptNo the right field for the certificate/license number we hold as free text? Any format constraints?

  4. Do we need exemption certificate management (ECM/CertCapture) for calculation to behave correctly, or is that only for audit documentation?

5 · Discounts, fees & credits

  1. Line-level discounts: should we send the net line amount, or gross plus a separate negative discount line? Does the choice affect the taxable-base breakdown you return?

  2. Do different discount types need different treatment — manual discounts vs promotions/coupons? In states that tax the pre-coupon amount for seller-reimbursed coupons, how should the transaction be represented?

  3. Line-level fees (a fee attached to a specific line, where the fee may be taxable even when the item is not): own line with the fee's tax code, or folded into the item line?

    Our lean: own line, so a taxable fee on a non-taxable plant is taxed correctly — confirm.

  4. Store credit applied to an order: as a discount (reduces taxable base) vs as a payment (tax-neutral) — is that distinction correct in all states? If a credit-as-discount lands after the invoice is paid, what's the compliant flow?

6 · Sourcing: pickup vs delivery

  1. For customer pickup at the nursery (including POS sales), is the pickup/register location's address the correct jurisdiction source in all states? Any origin-sourcing states where delivery orders also source from our customer's location?

  2. We send header-level ship-from per fulfilling location. Anything else needed for correct sourcing when a nursery later operates multiple locations?

7 · Registration & home-rule jurisdictions

For the initial release, where an org is registered lives in Plantiful — nexus is not declared in Avalara.

  1. Which endpoints should we use to source the list of US states, and the self-administered / home-rule sub-jurisdictions per state (AL, CO, LA, AK…), for our registration-picker UI?

  2. When a destination is in a home-rule state where the org is registered only in some local jurisdictions, we plan to call AvaTax and then suppress the tax for jurisdictions the org isn't registered in. How do we reliably match the authority identifiers in your response to the jurisdiction list from the endpoint above?

    Sanity check welcome: is response-side suppression the right pattern here, or is there a request-side way to scope which jurisdictions apply without declaring nexus?

  3. When the destination state isn't one the org is registered in, we skip the call and apply $0. Any concerns with that approach from your side?

8 · Operations & limits

  1. What are the rate limits and expected latency for CreateTransaction? What call-ratio-per-order do you consider healthy? (We plan to debounce to roughly 3–5 calls per order.)

  2. What's the recommended way to monitor service health — status page, webhooks, error codes we should treat as retryable vs terminal?

  3. Should we send the X-Avalara-Client header even though we're not pursuing certification initially? What format do you want from us?

  4. Is the official JS SDK (avatax on npm, AvaTax-REST-V2-JS-SDK) the recommended client for Node backends? Anything about its maintenance cadence we should know?

9 · Looking ahead (not blocking)

  1. When we move to committed transactions + declared nexus for remittance/reporting later: anything in the calculator-only architecture above that would make that migration painful?

  2. What does the Sales Tax Calculation Badge / certification path look like for an integration structured as a single partner account with per-customer companies?
