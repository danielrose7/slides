The v1 calculation loop

tax-basis change→ flush / debounce window→ pre-checks (entitlement · registration · exempt-tracking)→ AvalaraCallRecord (request)→ CreateTransaction (SalesOrder)→ AvalaraCallRecord (response / failure)→ per-jurisdiction breakdown + display

Settled decisions

  • Calculator-only v1 — every call DocType: SalesOrder: unrecorded, ~1/10 cost, nothing to void on abandonment
  • Nexus syncs to Avalara — no declared nexus ⇒ $0 everywhere; Plantiful registration config is the source of truth, mirrored per company
  • Tax date — invoice date, ship-date fallback; a date change recalcs like any line edit
  • Net line amounts — discounts + credits pre-applied via a rounding-safe distribution util; third-party coupons excepted (negative OC030000 line, pre-coupon base)
  • Partial exemption — header entityUseCode + per-line taxCode; ag-exempt customers still taxed on non-qualifying lines
  • No NT short-circuit — send real tax codes; Avalara decides taxability
  • Fully-paid boundary — no new credits after full payment; money-back is a return/refund, never a credit-recalc
  • Lookups bill at ~1/10 rate — cache tax-code and jurisdiction lists server-side; never call per keystroke

Open items

All tracked on the project board

  • Every remaining verification and open design decision is captured as an acceptance criterion on its owning ticket in the Sales Tax v2 (Avalara +) Linear project — sandbox checks on the client scaffold, docCode uniqueness on the entitlement ticket, home-rule scoping on the registration ticket, the POS fallback amount on the POS ticket, and client-header variants on the sandbox setup ticket. Nothing is pending outside the board.
  • Returns were punted for v1 entirely (estimates-only) — scaffolding lives on the returns ticket for the commit era (see lifecycle).
